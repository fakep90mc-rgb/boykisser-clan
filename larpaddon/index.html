Basefinding

11 modules for spotting chunks players have touched on DonutSMP: four generations of chunkfinder, sus chunk and amethyst chunk finder, amethyst and anti-ESP bypasses, player bypass v3 that fingerprints chunks and re-checks whether they keep ticking, plus light and mei mei debug.

Misc

24 modules around the hunt: better block ESP, geode ESP checked against the seed, hole and rotated deepslate ESP, netherite bypass v3 for ancient debris, auto mine, auto trident, legit auto tool, elytra helper, freecam, protect, fake roles, stats changer, and the home, rtp, relog and pearl methods.

HUD

Three HUD elements: a rain bucket that shows the weather where you stand, a DonutSMP region map with your spot on it, and a staff list.

Policy

Your account

Logging in with Discord gives the site your user ID, username, display name and avatar. The site also stores when you first logged in, when you were last seen and how many times you logged in. That is what your public profile at /profiles/<your id> shows. Nothing else is read from your Discord account, and the site never posts or messages on your behalf.

If the community server's bot is running, your profile also shows your standing in the community Discord: in the server, muted, banned or not a member. This is asked from Discord when the profile is opened. A ban reason is only shown to you and to staff.

Uploads

Configs must be Meteor .nbt files under 200 KB with more than 5 modules enabled; packs must be resource pack .zip files. Everything you upload waits in a queue until staff approve it, and staff can reject or delete it at any time without giving a reason. Uploading malware, stealers, spam or anything that harms other players gets your account banned from the site and blacklisted from uploading.

Staff see flags on configs that contain a spam module, macros or /pay commands. Previews of configs and texture packs are unpacked in your own browser; nothing is sent anywhere for that.

Appeals

If you are banned or muted in the community Discord you can send one appeal per punishment from your profile. What you write is stored and shown to staff. A denied appeal means a seven day wait before you can appeal that punishment again, and staff can blacklist an account from appealing.

Moderation

Every staff action (approving, rejecting or deleting uploads, handling appeals, blacklisting, unbanning, unmuting, role changes) is written to a log that staff can read, with the staff member's name and the Discord ID it concerned.

Counters and statistics

Download counters count each logged-in member once by their ID and each guest once by a salted hash of their address. Visitor statistics use a salted hash of address and browser, keep a visitor once per hour and are deleted after 400 days. The raw address is never stored, and previews don't count as downloads.

Cookies and storage

One cookie keeps you logged in for up to 30 days and is removed when you log out. Your browser's session storage remembers whether you dismissed the log-in prompt on the Download page. There are no analytics, ads or trackers, and link previews are blocked for this site.

Third parties

Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and avatars from Discord's CDN, so those services see your address. Logging in goes through Discord. Some downloads under Other Mods come straight from Modrinth or the mod's own site.

Limits

To stop abuse, uploads, appeals, profile lookups and log-ins are rate limited. Hammering the site gets you blocked.

Deleting your data

Ask in the community Discord and your account record, uploads and appeals will be deleted.

Contact

Questions, takedowns or a ban you want to talk about: the community Discord.

Before anything else: install Rat Poison

Rat Poison from Other Mods stops the token grabbers and RATs that get bundled into fake mods before they can run. Install it now, not after.

Think you got ratted? Do this in order

  1. Download and run silent-net-remover.bat. It checks your PC for silent net. If nothing is there it just tells you so and closes; only if it finds it does it clean up and restart Windows.
    Download silent-net-remover.batopen source
  2. Run a full scan with Malwarebytes, and/or a scan with Bitdefender, and remove whatever they find.
  3. Change every password you care about afterwards, Discord and Microsoft first.

Scan it first

Upload the jar in the Scanner tab, or at ratscanner.org. It tells you whether the mod is a known rat and what it does.

Obfuscated?

If the scanner cannot read it because it is obfuscated, run it in a throwaway VM like tria.ge and watch what it does with Wireshark and/or Procmon.

Silent net cleaner

If the scan says silent net, drop the jar here. The cleaner by sub5larp is built into this page: nothing is uploaded, the rat is stripped out in your browser and you get the jar back with -cleaned in its name.
